# Side Hustle Scripts

A grab-bag of small tools for people running side hustles: gig-app math,
client-finder utilities, and payout trackers.

## What is in here
- `gig-math.py` - true hourly pay for driving/gig work after fees, gas, and wear
- `client-emails.py` - cost-ranked list of ways to find + verify client emails before cold outreach
- `payout-audit.py` - feeds your payout history in, flags hidden fees and underpriced hours
